# HELLBREACH

A first-person shooter that sits between Doom and Halo. You get Doom's speed, gore, glory kills and arena fights, plus Halo's squadmates, radio chatter, objectives and story.

Open `index.html` in a browser. No server or install is needed, and three.js is vendored, so it also runs offline.

## What's new in this version

- **Modern graphics.** High-resolution textures with surface detail (normal maps), realistic lighting, smoother models, bloom, filmic colour grading and anti-aliasing. Settings has High/Medium/Low quality presets; the old 8-bit look is still available under "Pixel look".
- **Every map is a different place.** There are five architectures:
  - roofed industrial facilities with blast doors, light panels and pipes
  - towering cathedral and forge halls with stained glass, chandeliers and banners
  - irregular caverns under rock ceilings with stalactites
  - open-sky canyons and wastelands with cliffs, distant mountains and set pieces on the horizon
  - floating islands over a void or a lava sea, joined by bridges

  Each level also has its own materials, props, sky, weather (snow, ash, embers, spores) and light.
- **Cutscenes.** Every mission opens with a cinematic: a dropship insertion on open-sky maps, or a push-in on the Warden indoors. Missions end with an outro shot. The prologue, interludes and ending play over rendered 3D scenes: Erebus and the Breach, the Long Vigil, the cryo pod, the gate and the Hollow Crown. Cutscenes can be turned off in Settings.
- **The story is in the levels.** Holographic *echoes* replay what happened in each place when you walk near them.
- **Harder and less predictable.** There is more spawn pressure, there are more waves and elites, and rooms can turn on you partway through:
  - a reinforcement portal
  - an invading champion
  - a swarm flood
  - a sniper nest
- **More lore.** World entries, plus a bestiary entry for every demon, unlocked on your first kill.

## The story

Erebus, 2291. A mining drill broke into something, and in 41 hours a colony of 30,000 was gone. You are the **Warden**, Subject Seven of Project LANTERN, a soldier rebuilt around a stolen demon heart.

**The overarching objective:** feed the LANTERN the burning hearts of the Warlords that anchor the Breach, then use it to seal the Breach. It turns out the Warlords were holding something else shut.

- **4 episodes, 20 missions.** Each has a briefing, its own objectives, radio dialogue during play, a boss with lines of its own, and a debrief.
- **Cast:** Commander Okafor (Overwatch), VESTA (your suit AI), Cpl. "Rook" Vasquez and Fireteam Ash, Dr. Tomas Reyne, and the Warlords themselves.
- **Story moments:** a prologue, interludes between episodes, a mid-story betrayal, a sacrifice, and an ending.
- **Codex:** 36 lore entries, plus an intel datapad (audio log) hidden in every mission.

## Missions are not just "kill everything"

Every room in a mission has an objective. The room types are:

| Objective | What you do |
|---|---|
| Rescue | Free captives from cages. They run for the evac point and the Host chases them. Some captives are marines who join your squad. |
| Escort | Keep a crawler full of colonists (or Dr. Reyne) alive. It only moves while you stay close, and it stalls halfway. |
| Sabotage | Hold position at consoles, valves, mirror arrays, or plant charges (they explode, so get clear). |
| Defend / Hold out | Protect an uplink, the Lantern or Reyne while a timer runs. In a hold-out you must stay inside the ring, and a dropship arrives at the end. |
| Capture | Stand in zones until they're yours. Demons inside contest the zone. |
| Retrieve | Carry a core to a receptacle. You are slowed, can't dash, and a hard hit makes you drop it. |
| Assassinate | Hunt named lieutenants who taunt you. |
| Purge the rot | Burn nests while a corruption meter climbs. When it's full it eats through your suit. |
| Blackout | The lights die and stalkers come. You only have your flashlight. |
| Gauntlet | Run through falling slag to the far gate. |
| *(original)* | Purge, Hell Gates, Survive, Elite Ambush |

Missions also have level modifiers: a fully dark mine, white-out fog, low gravity in the Void Spires, falling slag, and a timed escape after the Magma Foundry boss.

**Fireteam Ash** fights alongside you in several missions. They follow you, shoot, take hits and go down. Stand next to a downed marine to revive them. Demons will target your allies, survivors and objectives as well as you.

**Checkpoints:** clearing a room saves your progress. If you die, you can retry from the last cleared room.

## Bosses

- **Cinematic intros:** the camera swings to the boss, it roars, a name card appears and the boss speaks. Your squad and VESTA call out how to fight it.
- **Break meter:** headshots, explosives and melee fill the gold bar under the boss's health bar. When it breaks, the boss is stunned. Get close and press **F** for a **Glory Strike**, which does heavy damage and heals you.
- **Signature mechanics:**
  - The Butcher's chain hook drags you in, and it eats corpses to heal. Interrupt it.
  - The Forge Tyrant floods the floor with magma. Get on high ground.
  - Vharos splits off shadow echoes and sets the throne room floor on fire.
  - The Pale Knight does a three-hit blade dance.
  - The Hollow God's gaze hits you if you're in its line of sight. The final fight ends with the Lantern igniting.
- **Slow motion on death:** each boss gets last words, and Warlords drop their heart.

## Dead Signal (horror campaign)

A separate five-chapter horror story, found in the **MODES** tab. A distress signal is coming out of Helix Site 9, the lab where the Warden's heart was taken two years ago. The voice on the radio belongs to a scientist who has been dead the whole time.

- **Atmosphere:**
  - Total darkness: you only have a flashlight.
  - Close fog and a desaturated, grainy picture.
  - No music: just a low drone, your heartbeat, whispers, banging, screams and lights that flicker out.
- **The Pale Mother:** an unkillable thing that only moves when you are not looking at it. The heartbeat speeds up as she gets closer.
- **Hard:**
  - Demons have more health and hit harder.
  - Half the pickups.
  - No squad.
  - More mid-room twists.
- **Rewards:**
  - Double coins while playing.
  - On each chapter's first clear: big coins, XP, a skill point and a legendary relic.
  - Surviving all five unlocks the **LAZARUS REVOLVER** and the Mother's Heart relic.

## Modes outside the story

- **Survival:** endless waves, the original mode.
- **Horde:** survival with eight **Skulls** (Iron, Famine, Thunderstorm, Tilt, Fury, Blackout, Glass, Mythic). Each skull makes the game harder and multiplies your score.
- **Boss Rush:** every boss of an episode back to back, or all 20, with best times saved.
- **Daily Operation:** one arena per day, generated from the date, with three skulls and a mutator. Survive 10 waves.
- **Descent:** a roguelite. You go down through random multi-room floors, each room with a random objective. After every floor you pick one of three boons. You get one life, and there's a Warlord every fifth floor.

Everything from the original game is still here: the armory, upgrades, skills, relics, trials, achievements, weapon mastery and all the settings.

## Controls

`WASD` move · mouse aim · click fire · right-click zoom · `Space` jump · `Shift` sprint · `R` reload · `E` dash · `X`/`C` powers · `Z` rage · `G` grenade · `F` melee, finisher or glory strike · `1`–`9` or wheel to switch weapon · `Esc` pause. Keys can be rebound in Settings. On phones, on-screen touch controls appear.

Objectives that need you to hold position fill up while you stand next to them, as long as no demon is in the way. There is no interact key.

## Project layout

```
index.html            page markup + script order
css/game.css          core HUD and menu styles
css/story.css         comms, cinematics, objectives, briefing, codex, modes
vendor/three.min.js   three.js r128 (MIT)
vendor/post/          three.js post-processing passes (MIT)
js/core/              the original engine, split by system
  engine.js           helpers, weapons data, save, audio, renderer, textures
  demons.js           enemy + boss data, levels, music
  player.js           player, viewmodel, particles, enemies, projectiles, pickups
  world.js            room-based level generation, navigation, hazards
  game.js             game state, spawning, AI, bosses
  upgrades.js         upgrades, abilities
  loop.js             main loop, HUD, end of run
  settings.js         settings, damage numbers, health bars
  ai.js               special demons, boss bodies and attacks
  run.js              run start, rooms and encounters
  trials.js, trials2.js, weapons2.js, progression.js, input.js, minimap.js
js/story/
  cast.js             characters, pixel portraits, synthesized voices
  comms.js            radio dialogue, cinematic camera, waypoint markers
  script.js           the campaign: missions, dialogue, boss lines, codex, ending
js/systems/
  actors.js           humans, allies (Fireteam Ash), soft targets, pathing
  missions.js         the room objectives, escape, environment, checkpoints, HUD
  bosses.js           intros, break meter and glory strike, signature attacks
  codex.js            codex and intel datapads
  modes.js            Horde, Boss Rush, Daily, Descent
js/modes/horror.js    Dead Signal: chapters, the stalker, atmosphere, rewards
js/ui/menu.js         story flow (prologue → briefing → debrief → ending), tabs
tools/bundle.py       builds dist/hellbreach.html, a single-file copy
```

Scripts are plain (non-module) files that share one global scope, so they also work from `file://`. Later files extend earlier ones by wrapping their functions, which is the same pattern the original single file used. The order in `index.html` matters.

To make a single-file copy to share:

```bash
python3 tools/bundle.py
```

## Saves

Progress is stored in the browser's `localStorage`, under the same key the original single-file version used, so existing progress carries over.
